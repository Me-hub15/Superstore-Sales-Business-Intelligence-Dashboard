 # Superstore Sales & Business Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customers, products, geographic performance, and shipping trends using the Superstore dataset.

The project transforms raw transactional data into an interactive business intelligence solution that helps identify key performance indicators, sales patterns, profitable products, customer behavior, regional performance, and shipping trends.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard consists of five analytical pages:

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of overall business performance.

**Key KPIs:**

* Total Sales: 2.30M
* Total Profit: 286.40K
* Profit Margin: 12%
* Total Orders: 2K
* Total Customers: 793

**Visual Analysis:**

* Regional Sales Performance
* Monthly Profit Trend
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Sales Mix by Category & Segment

---

### 2. Product Analysis

Focuses on product and category-level performance.

**Key Analysis:**

* Top Products by Profit
* Category and Sub-Category performance
* Sales, Profit and Quantity analysis
* Profit Margin by category and sub-category
* Product-level filtering
* Category and Segment filtering

This page helps identify high-performing products as well as products and sub-categories with weaker profitability.

---

### 3. Customer Analysis

Analyzes customer segments and individual customer performance.

**Key Analysis:**

* Sales Distribution by Segment
* Top 10 Customers by Sales
* Product-level sales and profit analysis
* Customer filtering
* Profit Margin analysis

The analysis highlights the contribution of Consumer, Corporate and Home Office segments and identifies the highest-value customers.

---

### 4. Geographic Analysis

Examines sales and profitability across different geographic regions and states.

**Key Analysis:**

* Top 10 States by Sales
* Sales Mix by Category
* Sales vs Profit by Region
* Geographic Sales Distribution
* State-level filtering
* Regional comparison

The page helps identify high-performing states and understand regional differences in sales and profitability.

---

### 5. Shipping Analysis

Analyzes order distribution and sales performance across different shipping modes.

**Shipping Modes:**

* Standard Class
* Second Class
* First Class
* Same Day

**Key Analysis:**

* Order Distribution by Ship Mode
* Sales Trend Over Time
* Monthly Sales Mix by Shipping Mode
* Shipping Mode Sales Trend
* Ship Mode filtering

This page provides insights into the distribution and sales contribution of different shipping methods.

## 📌 Key Insights

Some important observations from the dashboard include:

* Total sales are approximately **2.30M**, with total profit of approximately **286.40K**.
* Overall profit margin is approximately **12%**.
* The **West region** contributes the highest sales among the four regions.
* **Technology** contributes the largest share of sales among the three major categories.
* The **Consumer segment** accounts for the largest share of sales compared with Corporate and Home Office.
* **California** is the highest-selling state in the dataset.
* A small number of products and customers contribute significantly to overall sales and profit.
* **Standard Class** is the dominant shipping mode by order volume.
* Sales and profit fluctuate considerably across different months and years.

> Note: These insights are based on the current dashboard calculations and may change when filters are applied.
